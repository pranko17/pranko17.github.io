Pixtangle: Pixel Art Puzzle

A relaxing Shikaku logic puzzle. Slice the grid into rectangles — every rectangle holds exactly one number, and that number is its area in cells. Fill the whole board and it blooms into a pixel art. The game works fully offline and needs no account.
